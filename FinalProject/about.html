About

Why this project exists and who it helps.

Problem

Students often miss local events because event information is scattered across different sites, and filters can be clunky or confusing.

Solution

This web app helps students find nearby events with simple search and filters, and lets users save events they are interested in.

Stakeholders

  • College students
  • Campus organizations
  • Local attendees
  • Event hosts who want more outreach
